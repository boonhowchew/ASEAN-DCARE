The argument, in one paragraph

Every member should be able to give this in under a minute. It is the answer to the most likely structural challenge: “isn’t this two projects?”

Effective psychosocial interventions for diabetes distress already exist in the literature. ASEAN health systems still do not scale them, and the reason is evidential: the regional evidence base is fragmented, largely unregistered, inconsistently reported, rarely replicated, and almost never contextualised to the settings where care is delivered. Ministries cannot act on it.

So we do two things at once. We generate one high-quality, pre-registered, culturally co-designed multi-country trial — and we build the regional machinery that makes such trials repeatable after we finish. The trial is the demonstration case; the ecosystem work is what makes it generalise. Fund only the trial and the region gets one more result it cannot use. Fund only the ecosystem work and you have infrastructure with nothing running through it.

What remains after month 60

  • Regional Quality Research Alliance — the sustainability mechanism. UPM hosts the first secretariat, with rotation; after 2031 it runs on membership fees, consultancy and symposium revenue
  • Five Research Consultation Clinics — one at every partner, operating from M18, for protocol development, peer review, reporting and mentorship
  • Nineteen researchers — 6 PhD, 6 postdoctoral and 7 Research Coordinator fellows; six of the seven coordinators sit outside the Hub
  • Reusable tools — assessment instruments, open protocols, reporting templates, bias-prevention resources
  • Registries — owned by the institutions that built them, usable for research not yet imagined
