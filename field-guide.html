Behind the screens: six layers that keep the AI bounded
Source: DREAM4HEALTH AI-powered digital intervention specification v0.1 (5 October 2026), building on application IA-0000000254 and the VEMOFIT trials (Chew et al., Diabet Med 2018; Ther Adv Endocrinol Metab 2019). Screens and example data are illustrative; the final design follows co-design with each country's Lived Experience Advisory Board (M24–30). Draft for consortium review.
01 Language, offline and voice
Works offline: modules download and progress syncs later. Five study languages, co-designed with each Lived Experience Advisory Board. Voice input where testing in that language passes.
FIXEDPROJECTION · voice
03 Six core modules, about 12 weeks
One module a fortnight: a 5–10-minute audio or video, one practice and one reflection. Psychoeducation, emotion regulation, values-based goals, self-monitoring. Booster at about 3 months, as in VEMOFIT.
FIXED · titles illustrative
05 Companion: Lifestyle and Self-Management Coach
Small daily steps tied to the person's own values and goals. At most one or two prompts a day at chosen times; quiet hours respected. Answers questions only from approved content.
FIXEDPROPOSED · prompt limits
07 Talk to my nurse
One tap asks the named nurse or coordinator to make contact. Human attention is the ingredient that sustained improvement to 12 months in VEMOFIT.
FIXED
02 Weekly check-in
Two distress questions (for example the DDS-2) and a mood rating. A safety question at set points: any positive answer goes straight to the fixed safety rules (layer 1 below), whatever the other scores.
PROPOSED
04 Companion: Emotional Support
Helps the person name what they feel (medication burden, family strain, fear of complications), reflects it back and guides a short skill from Modules 2–3. Always says it is an AI programme. Never diagnoses, advises on medication or counsels a crisis: risk goes to a person.
FIXEDM24 GATE · model
06 Self-monitoring logs
Optional: medicine-taking, phone step count, meals, and home glucose if already measured. Feeds the coach and the progress view; never used to adjust treatment.
PROPOSED
08 Safety flags
Tier 3 (crisis signal): scripted crisis reply on the phone and same-day clinician contact. Tier 2: contact within an agreed time. Tier 1: routine queue.
PROPOSED · tiersDECIDE · responders
09 Contacts due
Scheduled human-attention contacts: active listening at visits and between them, for example fortnightly in weeks 1–12 and monthly to month 12.
FIXEDDECIDE · cadence
10 Engagement at a glance
One line per person: modules done, check-ins completed, last active. Designed for under 10 minutes a day; no transcripts on the main view.
PROPOSED
11 Questions for the next visit
Questions the person saved in the app, shared with consent, so the consultation starts where they are.
PROPOSED
12 AI status and audit
Shows the frozen model version (M30 pilot, M36 definitive trial), the log-review queue and a central pause switch.

Rules outside the AI (safety question, crisis phrases in each language) trigger escalation directly.

Orchestration
Chooses the module, prompt or skill; enforces dose limits, quiet hours and boundaries.

Converses only within approved content. Chosen at the M24 gate; frozen for each trial.
