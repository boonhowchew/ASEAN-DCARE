What this map contains

Two evidence bases

The Experimental and Non-experimental tabs each hold a curated core (appraised in detail, with effect estimates or associations) and the full PubMed landscape for that stream.

Instruments

The Instruments tab lists the DD questionnaire families, their origin papers, and every language version whose validation appears in this corpus, with links.

Geography and guidance

The Map tab maps the 248 trials pooled by the 2026 EASD review by study country, and the corpus by author-affiliation country. The Guideline tab holds the EASD 2026 recommendations, the ADA Standards of Care and position statement, and ISPAD, with a side-by-side comparison.

        Scope and limitations. This is a research and teaching reference, not clinical advice, and it does not replace the judgement of a qualified health professional or the full text of any guideline. Study-design labels across the 2,438-record landscape were assigned with model assistance by a single reviewer and are a navigational aid rather than a quality appraisal; the appraisals of the 17 core sources are also single-reviewer. Guideline wording is quoted verbatim from the editions named in each card — always check the current edition before applying it to care. Corrections and additions are welcome via the repository issue tracker.
