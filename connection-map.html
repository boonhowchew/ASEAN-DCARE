The patient's phone
Lessons, weekly check-ins and safety checks live on the phone and work without internet. Data are locked (encrypted) and sent when a signal returns.

The nurse's computer
Shows who needs a call today, who may be at risk, and questions patients want to ask. Can pause the AI for the whole clinic.

Existing health records
Local institution's diabetes registry and health records, SingHealth records, Indonesia's Prolanis. Used only with official approval, sought in Year 1.

Clinic tablets for new registers
In Myanmar and the Philippines, staff record patients on tablets at two clinics each, even without internet.

The app's “brain”
The AI that chats with patients, plus the library of approved answers it must stick to. Runs on a server inside the country, or on a national secure cloud.
CHOSEN AT MONTH 24
Automatic safety desk
A server that double-checks every warning sign, alerts the right nurse, keeps a full record for the safety committee, and removes names before anything is studied.
SUGGESTED
Tidy up and anonymise
Puts every source into the same format and scoring rules, like one shared template, then removes names and ID numbers. The data owner approves every release.
AGREEDUNDER DISCUSSION
The country's research database
Kept on a server in the country. Holds the new registers and the trial questionnaires; owned by the country's own team, who look at it first.
AGREEDSUGGESTED
Rules that cover everything
A signed data agreement (month 6), an AI ethics framework, an independent safety committee and scientific advisers. The partners decide together.

Where the AI “thinks”A mix of three places, chosen to keep personal talk private and the app working without internet.On the phone
A small AI built into the app for tips and reminders, even with no signal. Phones could already run such models in 2025.
FORECASTIn the country
A larger AI that understands local languages (for example the SEA-LION family: Burmese, Filipino, Indonesian, Malay), on a local server or national cloud.
CHOSEN AT MONTH 24Rented cloud, under contract
A commercial AI only where the law allows, with a contract that bans using our data to train it.
CHOSEN AT MONTH 24Safety never depends on the AI
Fixed rules on the phone and the server check every warning sign and every reply.
SUGGESTEDNever
Personal conversations do not cross country borders.
